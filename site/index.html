Player entry impact
Gold counts only their opening kills; blue counts every opening duel they were in, won or lost
Because every duel has a killer and a victim and exactly one side wins the round, the blue column is zero-sum across the lobby and lands on exactly 50% LAN-wide — so it reads straight off as above or below par. Gold flatters anyone who only gets measured on the duels they win; blue charges them for the ones they lose. Two rounds have no opening kill on record, so the denominator is {{ entry.entries }} rather than {{ meta.roundsTotal }}.
